Good to know

Kebab in Fatih, a short walk from Sultanahmet

Where can I eat good kebab near Sultanahmet and the Grand Bazaar?

Nasip Et is in Kumkapı, Fatih: 15 minutes on foot from the Grand Bazaar and 18 from the Blue Mosque. Guests rate us 4.9 out of 5 on Google, from more than 3,350 reviews.

Are you open late at night?

Yes. We are open 24 hours a day, every day of the week: for a late dinner, after a flight, or for a Turkish breakfast.

What should I order?

An Adana or Urfa kebab from the charcoal grill, a testi kebab broken open at your table, a pide from the oven and künefe to finish. With tea, of course.

Is there an English menu with prices?

Yes. The full menu with prices in Turkish lira is on this site in seven languages. Dish names stay in Turkish, so you can simply point and order.

Can I order delivery?

Yes, on Yemeksepeti, Trendyol Go and Migros Yemek.

Where exactly are you?

Çakmaktaşı Sk. No:6, Kumkapı, 34126 Fatih, İstanbul. Call or WhatsApp +90 542 576 41 41.
